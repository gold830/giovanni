MEET DEEJAY GIOVANNI

Faith at the centre. God at the wheel.

Born Jovan Ssempungu on October 9, 1996, Deejay Giovanni is a Ugandan gospel DJ, Christian rapper, songwriter and media personality based in Kampala.

Working across gospel deejaying, Christian rap and broadcasting, Giovanni brings together rhythm, creativity and a message rooted in faith. His work reaches beyond the decks, using music and media to connect with people through sounds that inspire and encourage.

For him, music is more than a performance. It is an opportunity to share a message, move a crowd and make the gospel heard in a language that connects with everyday life.

THE SOUND IS ONLY PART OF THE STORY. THE MESSAGE MATTERS.

Not just beats. A message.

Gospel music can move a room. It can also move a heart.

Giovanni's approach to gospel deejaying puts the message at the heart of the experience. Through Christian music and rap, his aim is to bring people together around sounds that carry hope, encouragement and faith.

Whether through a carefully built mix, a recorded song or a live set, the intention remains the same: to make music that people can enjoy while keeping its purpose in view.

THE MEDIA PERSONALITY

A voice beyond the speakers.

Giovanni's work extends into television and media, bringing his personality and passion for gospel music to a wider audience.

His media work has included platforms such as Dream TV and NBS TV, including involvement with NBS Mission 316. This side of his career adds another dimension to his connection with audiences — one built through communication as well as music.
